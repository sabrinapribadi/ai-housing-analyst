# ADR-006: Industry Alignment — Short-Term Rental Guest Experience

**Status:** Accepted (2026-11-11), before the P7–P11 runs
**Date:** 2026-11-11
**Deciders:** Sabrina Pribadi

---

## Context and Problem Statement

- Rule R16: the review-search feature must answer the questions short-term rental practitioners actually ask, in their terms.
- **Question:** who uses guest-review answers, for which decisions, under which rules, and what does a useful answer look like?

## What practitioners expect

| Expectation | What it means here | Source (R6) | How this project meets it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Rated categories** | Guests rate Cleanliness, Accuracy, Check-in, Communication, Location and Value, plus an overall rating that is not their average | Airbnb Help Center, "How ratings work" (vendor doc, read 2026-11-11) | The six categories are question aspects (configs/rag_eval.yaml) |
| **Text over stars** | Airbnb star ratings cluster near the top, so the written review carries the signal | Zervas, Proserpio and Byers 2021 (Marketing Letters) | Answers come from review text, not star averages |
| **Aspects hosts act on** | Review text reveals the drivers of satisfaction (room, staff, location, value) | Xiang et al. 2015 (Int. J. Hospitality Management) | Ten managed aspects added: noise, transport access, wifi, kitchen, space, families, luggage, bathroom, bed, air conditioning |
| **Regulation (Japan)** | Private Lodging Business Act: at most 180 nights a year; notification to the prefecture or Tokyo special ward; hosts keep the home sanitary, explain noise rules, handle neighbour complaints, keep a guest register | Japan Tourism Agency (MLIT) Minpaku portal, overview of the Act (public body, read 2026-11-11) | Noise and cleanliness are first-class aspects; answers flag complaints a host must act on |
| **Users and decisions** | Hosts fix recurring complaints; property managers compare listings; investors screen wards | Xiang et al. 2015; Act above (management operators for absent hosts) | Questions can be scoped to one ward; every claim cites a review id so it can be checked |
| **Multilingual guests** | Reviews in this snapshot: about 68% Latin script, 16% Chinese, 9% Japanese, 7% Korean (20,000-review sample) | Inside Airbnb Tokyo (measured here) | Retrieval candidates must be multilingual (ADR-008); answers are in English with cited originals |
| **Deliverable** | A short, quoted, checkable answer, not a score | Practice above | 2–5 sentences, a citation after each claim, a plain "the reviews do not cover this" when true |
| **Cadence** | Inside Airbnb publishes snapshots several times a year | Inside Airbnb (dataset) | One snapshot (29–30 Sep 2025); refresh is out of scope |

## Decision

- Frame review search as **"What do guests say about <aspect> [in <ward>]?"** over the six rated categories and ten managed aspects.
- Measure answers on what a host can trust: every sentence supported by a cited review, and an honest abstention when reviews are silent.

## Consequences

- **Positive:** questions and metrics match how hosts, managers and investors read reviews; noise and cleanliness map to legal duties.
- **Negative:** occupancy, ADR and RevPAR are not answerable from reviews; the calendar has no prices (ADR-005), so revenue questions stay out of scope.
