# ADR-003: Retrieval over Guest Reviews

**Status:** Accepted — written retrospectively on 2026-11-11 from the code and PRD; the decision dates from June 2026
**Date:** 2026-11-11
**Deciders:** Sabrina Pribadi

---

## Context and Problem Statement

- Hosts and analysts want to know what guests say about noise, cleanliness or access, by area.
- **Question:** how can the assistant answer from real reviews and show its sources?

## Decision Drivers

- Answers grounded in quoted reviews; one-time indexing cost under a dollar.

## Decision

- **Index:** 25,000 reviews embedded with OpenAI `text-embedding-3-small` at 256 dimensions, stored in ChromaDB (`scripts/build_review_index.py`); about $0.05 one time.
- **Answer:** the 10 nearest reviews go to `gpt-4o-mini` (temperature 0), which answers from them; sources are shown, auto-translated to English with the original kept (`src/agent/rag_agent.py`).

## Considered Options (SOTA comparison: tested here vs cited only, R2)

| Option | Tested here | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Embeddings + vector store (retrieval-augmented generation)** | **Yes (chosen)** | Finds reviews by meaning across languages |
| Keyword search | No | Misses paraphrases and other languages |
| Index all 444,182 reviews | No | About 18× the cost; 25,000 cover the questions asked |

## Consequences

- **Positive:** every answer shows the reviews it used.
- **Negative:** only 25,000 of 444,182 reviews are searchable; retrieval quality is not measured with a labelled set.
